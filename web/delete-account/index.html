Delete your Open Mic Finder account

This page permanently deletes an Open Mic Finder account. It works even if you have already uninstalled the app. You can also delete your account inside the app: Profile tab, Settings, Delete account.

What deletion removes

  • Your sign-in is removed entirely. The account can never be used again and your email address is freed.
  • Your profile is anonymized: display name, handle, photo, bio, social links, home area, and birth year are all erased.
  • Your favorites, saved preferences, push notification tokens, blocks, and performer details are deleted.
  • Producer contact details and payout references are deleted.

What is retained, and why

  • Anonymized signup and lineup history rows are kept so past events stay intact for other people. They no longer contain anything that identifies you: they point at a blank record labeled "Deleted user".
  • Content you reported, and moderation records about content, are kept for safety and legal reasons. They are not linked to your identity after deletion.

How long it takes

Deletion runs immediately when you confirm. Your sign-in stops working right away. Backup copies age out of our systems within 30 days.

Start deletion

Enter the email address on the account. We will send a confirmation link to prove the address is yours. No password needed.

Confirm deletion

Your email address is confirmed. This is the last step, and it cannot be undone.
