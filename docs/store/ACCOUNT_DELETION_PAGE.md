# Account Deletion Page (hostable web copy)

Google Play requires a public web page where users can request account
deletion without reinstalling the app (Play Console: App content, Data
deletion). Apple reviewers also look for an out-of-app deletion path. Host
this page and paste its URL into the Play Console form and the privacy
policy. The support address must match `src/lib/support.ts`.

---

# Delete your Open Mic Finder account

## The fast way: inside the app

Open the app, go to the Profile tab, then Settings, then Delete account.
Deletion is immediate and permanent.

## No longer have the app?

Email kyle@stonedgooseproductions.com from the address on your account with the
subject "Delete my account". We verify the request against the account email
and complete the deletion within 7 days, then confirm by reply.

## What gets deleted

- Your sign-in, email address, and password.
- Your profile: name, handle, bio, photo, links, birth year, and home area.
- Your push notification tokens, favorites, and blocks.
- Producer contact details, if you listed a mic.

## What is kept

Records of past signups are kept in anonymized form, with nothing personal
attached, so event history stays intact for the hosts who ran those nights.
Abuse reports keep an anonymized reference for moderation audit integrity.
Subscription billing history stays with Apple or Google under their own
policies; we cannot delete records they hold.
